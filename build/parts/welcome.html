Welcome to your Cheat Sheet 👋

This is your English companion from Learn with Noa. It does two jobs:

  • 📚
    A quick reference while you speak. Verb tenses, irregular verbs, grammar, phrases for when you're stuck… all short, clear and with Spanish where it helps.
  • 📝
    Your own English notebook. Save new words and class notes here, practice them with flashcards, and find them again anytime.

💡 Bookmark it now (Ctrl+D or ⌘D) so it's always one click away.

During class 🎧

Keep this tab open next to Zoom. When you need something, it's right here:

  • 🔍
    Search everything with the box at the top. Press / to jump to it. Try “since” or “make”.
  • 🆘
    Stuck? Open I'm stuck! in the menu for phrases that keep you talking in English.
  • ⇄
    Translator is pinned in the green menu, for the word you just can't find.
  • ★
    Star the pages you use most. They move to the top of the menu.

After class 📝

Five minutes here after each class makes a big difference:

  • +
    Save new words with the green + word button. It's on every page.
  • ✎
    Write your class notes on your Home page: corrections, things to practice, questions for next time.
  • 🃏
    Practice your words with flashcards. The ones you miss come back sooner.
  • ☀
    Check your Home page for a new word of the day and a thought for today.
