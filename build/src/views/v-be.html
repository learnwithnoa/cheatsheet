The verb to be

One verb, two meanings in Spanish: ser and estar. Use it for identity, feelings, location, age and descriptions.

In short
be = ser and estar. Use it for age and feelings too: I'm 30, I'm hungry (not "I have").

How you use it

Negative

  • I'm not tired.
  • She isn't here.
  • They weren't ready.

Questions: flip it

  • Are you ok?
  • Was it expensive?
  • Where is he from?

Age and feelings

  • I am 30. (no “I have 30”)
  • I'm hungry / cold / afraid.
  • tengo hambre → I'm hungry

There is / there are

  • There is a problem. (hay, singular)
  • There are two options. (hay, plural)
  • There was a party. (hubo/había)
