Job interviews

The questions you'll almost always hear, how to start your answers, and phrases that give you a few seconds to think.

🎯 Practice it: step-by-step rounds and a 🎤 practice interview where Hibi checks your English.

In short
Answer the question, give one real example, and connect it to the job. Short and specific beats long and perfect.

The classic questions

Tell me about yourself.

  • I'm a… with five years of experience in…
  • Right now, I work at… where I…
  • I'm especially good at…
  • That's why this role caught my attention.

Why do you want this job?

  • I've followed your company for a while, and…
  • This role is a great fit for my experience in…
  • I'm looking for a place where I can…

What are your strengths?

  • I'm very organized. For example, …
  • People often tell me I'm good at…
  • One of my strengths is…

What's your biggest weakness?

  • I used to…, but now I…
  • I'm working on… by…
  • Sometimes I…, so I've started to…

Where do you see yourself in five years?

  • I'd like to grow into a role where I…
  • I see myself leading…
  • I want to keep learning about…

Why should we hire you?

  • I have the experience you need in…
  • I can bring…
  • I've already done this kind of work at…

Tell a story: the STAR method

For "Tell me about a time when…" questions. Use the past simple for the story.

S · Situation

  • At my last job, we had a problem with…
  • A few months ago, …

T · Task

  • My job was to…
  • I needed to…

A · Action

  • First, I… Then, I…
  • I decided to…
  • I talked to… and we…

R · Result

  • In the end, we…
  • As a result, sales went up 20%.
  • I learned that…

Talk about your experience

Until now

  • I've worked in sales for six years.
  • I've been at my company since 2021.
  • I've led teams of up to ten people.

In the past

  • I was responsible for…
  • I managed a budget of…
  • I worked closely with…

Buy some time

Think

  • That's a great question.
  • Let me think about that for a second.
  • Good question. Let me give you an example.

Check

  • Sorry, could you repeat the question?
  • Do you mean…?
  • Just to make sure I understand, …

Your questions for them

Ask

  • What does a typical day look like in this role?
  • How do you measure success in this position?
  • What are the next steps in the process?

Finish

  • Thank you for your time.
  • I really enjoyed our conversation.
  • I look forward to hearing from you.
I work here since 2021 → I've worked here since 2021. And I'm agree → I agree. Two classics in interviews!
