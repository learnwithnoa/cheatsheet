Countable, uncountable & quantifiers

First ask: can I count it? One apple, two apples = countable. Water, information, advice, money = uncountable (always singular).

In short
Can you count it? Countable → many, a few. Uncountable (water, advice, money) → much, a little. A lot of works for both.
