Verb tenses

Type any verb and pick a subject. All 12 tenses update for you, with the affirmative (+), negative (−) and question (?) forms.

In short
Simple = facts and habits · continuous (be + -ing) = in progress · perfect (have + participle) = connected to a later moment.

Be going to + verb is the other future you'll use all the time. Use it for plans you already decided: I'm going to study tonight. Will es más para decisiones en el momento: “I'll help you!”
