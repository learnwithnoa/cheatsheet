Copying from a spreadsheet? You can also import it exactly as it is
  1. Open the spreadsheet or document where you keep your words.
  2. Select the rows with your words (the English, Spanish and example columns) and copy them (Ctrl+C / ⌘C).
  3. Paste them in the box above and press Import exactly as pasted. No AI, every row becomes one word.
