Passive voice

Use the passive when the action matters more than who did it, or when you don't know who did it. Structure: be + past participle. Only the verb be changes tense.

In short
be + past participle. Use it when the action matters more than who did it: My phone was stolen.
active

Someone stole my phone.

passive

My phone was stolen.

Tensebe + participleExample
Present simpleam / is / are + madeWine is made from grapes.
Present continuousis / are being + madeThe road is being repaired.
Past simplewas / were + madeThis house was built in 1920.
Present perfecthas / have been + madeThe meeting has been cancelled.
Futurewill be + madeYou will be contacted soon.
Modalscan / must / should be + madeThis form must be signed.

Good to know

Who did it? Use by

Don Quixote was written by Cervantes.

Only add "by" if it's important or new information.
