Emails & meetings

Copy-paste-ready phrases for work. Neutral and professional, not stiff.

In short
Start friendly, say why you're writing, ask clearly, and close with a next step.

Emails

Start

  • Hi Sarah,
  • Dear Mr. Smith, (formal)
  • I hope you're doing well.
  • Thanks for your email.
  • I'm writing to…

Ask for something

  • Could you please send me…?
  • Would it be possible to…?
  • I'd appreciate it if you could…
  • Let me know if… works for you.

Follow up

  • Just following up on my last email.
  • I wanted to check in about…
  • Any updates on…?
  • Just a quick reminder that…

Attach and share

  • Please find attached…
  • I've attached the…
  • Here's the link to…
  • As discussed, …

Apologize

  • Sorry for the late reply.
  • Apologies for the confusion.
  • Thanks for your patience.

Finish

  • Let me know if you have any questions.
  • Looking forward to hearing from you.
  • Best, · Best regards, · Thanks,

Meetings

Join in

  • Can I add something?
  • Sorry to interrupt, but…
  • Just to build on what Ana said, …

Clarify

  • Could you clarify what you mean by…?
  • Just to make sure we're on the same page, …
  • So the next step is…, right?

Online problems

  • Sorry, you're on mute.
  • You froze for a second.
  • Can you see my screen?
  • I think my connection is bad.

Wrap up

  • Let's circle back on this next week.
  • I'll send a quick summary.
  • Thanks, everyone. Talk soon.
I wait your answer → I look forward to your reply. And it's looking forward to hearing, with -ing, not to hear.
