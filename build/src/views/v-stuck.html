I'm stuck!

Don't switch to Spanish. Use one of these and keep going. Quedarse pegado es normal; lo importante es salir en inglés.

In short
Stay in English: describe it (It's a kind of…), buy time (Let me think…) or ask (How do you say…?).

You don't know a word

  • How do you say … in English?
  • What's the word for…?
  • I don't know the word, but…

Explain it another way

  • It's a kind of…
  • It's something you use to…
  • It's like a…, but…
  • It's the opposite of…
  • It's a person who…

Buy some time

  • Let me think…
  • How can I put this…
  • Give me a second.
  • Well, it's hard to explain, but…

You didn't understand

  • Sorry, can you say that again?
  • Could you speak a little slower?
  • What does … mean?
  • Sorry, I didn't catch that.

Check that you understood

  • So you mean that…?
  • Do you mean…?
  • Just to be sure, …

You made a mistake

  • Sorry, I mean…
  • Wait, let me say that again.
  • That came out wrong.
Example: I need a… how can I put this… it's something you use to open a bottle of wine. → a corkscrew! (sacacorchos)
