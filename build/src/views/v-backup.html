Back up & move

✓
You're signed in, so you don't need this page. Everything already syncs to any device where you sign in with Google.

Not signed in? Your words, class notes and starred sections live only in this browser. Use this page to save a backup, or to move everything to another computer, browser or your phone. Or just and it all syncs on its own.

step 1 · on this device

Copy your backup code

Then paste it somewhere you can open on the other device: an email to yourself, Google Keep, a Google Doc or a chat with yourself.

step 2 · on the other device

Paste it here

Open the Cheat Sheet on the other device, come to this page (Back up or move your notes, at the bottom of any page) and paste the code.

Restoring adds to what's already on that device. It never deletes anything, and words or notes that are already there won't be duplicated. Do this again whenever you want the other device to catch up.
