In short
Most verbs just add -ed. Irregular ones change: go → went → gone. Column 2 is the past (I went), column 3 goes after have (I've gone).
Where you see two forms (learned / learnt), the first one is American English and the second is British. Both are correct.

Regular verbs: how to say -ed

/t/

After p, k, f, s, sh, ch, x

asked, cooked, danced, finished, helped, liked, stopped, walked, washed, worked

“walked” suena como “walkt”. Una sola sílaba.

/d/

After vowels and voiced sounds

answered, arrived, changed, cleaned, enjoyed, listened, lived, opened, played, studied

“played” = “pleid”. Tampoco agregas sílaba.

/ɪd/

Only after t or d

accepted, ended, expected, needed, planted, started, visited, waited, wanted

Solo aquí aparece la sílaba extra: “wan-tid”, “nee-did”.
