Common mistakes

The mistakes Spanish speakers make most often, and how to fix them. Everyone makes these, so don't worry. Just try to notice them.

In short
The big ones: I'm 30 (not "I have 30"), I agree (not "I'm agree"), explain to me (not "explain me"). Notice them, don't panic.
