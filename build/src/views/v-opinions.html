Giving opinions

Ways to say what you think, agree, and disagree without sounding rude.

In short
I think… · I see your point, but… · I'm not sure about that. Disagree with the idea, not the person.

Give your opinion

  • I think…
  • In my opinion, …
  • Personally, I feel that…
  • For me, …
  • If you ask me, …

Agree

  • I totally agree.
  • Exactly!
  • That's a good point.
  • I feel the same way.
  • Same here.

Partly agree

  • I see your point, but…
  • That's true, but…
  • It depends.
  • Yes and no.
  • I agree up to a point.

Disagree politely

  • I'm not sure about that.
  • I see it a bit differently.
  • I don't really agree, because…
  • Maybe, but have you thought about…?

Ask for opinions

  • What do you think?
  • How do you feel about…?
  • What's your take on…?
  • Do you agree?

Not sure

  • I've never thought about it.
  • I'm not sure, to be honest.
  • I don't have a strong opinion.
Careful: I agree · I am agree. Agree is a verb, so no am. Same with I don't agree.
