Adverbs of frequency

How often do you do it?

In short
always, usually, often, never go before the main verb but after be: I always study · He is always late.

Where it goes

Before the main verb

I always study after class.
She never eats meat.

After the verb to be

He is always happy.
They are usually late.

Longer expressions go at the end

I go to the gym twice a week.
We meet every Friday.
