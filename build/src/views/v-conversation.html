Keep the conversation going

A conversation is not a job interview. Answer, add a little extra, and ask something back. Conectar es más importante que responder perfecto.

In short
Answer, add a little extra, then ask something back. That's the whole trick.

React

Really? · That's cool! · Oh wow! · I see. · No way! · Sounds fun! · Are you serious? · That must have been amazing! · Oh, I didn't know that!

Ask back

What about you? · How was it? · And then what happened? · Who did you go with? · How long have you been doing that? · Would you recommend it? · What's your favorite part about it?

Connect ideas

Speaking of that… · That reminds me… · Actually… · By the way… · Funny you mention that… · Now that you say that…

Interview vs real conversation

interview

How was your weekend?

Good.

…

conversation

How was your weekend?

It was good, thanks! I went to the movies with some friends. Have you watched anything good recently?

Yeah, I saw the new Dune movie.

Oh really? I wanted to see that! Was it good?
